# Enable TMDb in H&M Player

TMDb adds ratings and vote counts, localised titles and short summaries, genres, runtime, age ratings, director/creator, six lead-cast photos, production company/country, movie budget and box office, and streaming availability for your selected country.

It is optional. It does not change or unlock IPTV streams, does not contact your IPTV provider and does not consume an IPTV connection.

## Get a key

1. Create or sign in to your account at [The Movie Database](https://www.themoviedb.org/).
2. Open [TMDb API settings](https://www.themoviedb.org/settings/api).
3. Accept the terms and create a developer API key.
4. Copy the 32-character **API Key**. H&M Player also accepts the longer API Read Access Token, but the short key is easier to enter with a TV remote.
5. Open **H&M Player → Settings → TMDb**.
6. Enter the key and choose **Save and test**.
7. Select the country where you live under **Content region**. Streaming services are shown for this region; it is independent from the interface, audio and subtitle languages.

The key is stored locally on the TV. Do not publish your key in screenshots, issues or public repositories.
